THREE WAYS TO PLAY

Start wherever you want. You do not have to use every feature.

🎙️

VOICE + SOUNDS

VOICE LAB → CRAZY MIXER
  1. 1Create your voice. Type the script, choose the voice and speed, then click GENERATE VOICE.
  2. 2Open it in Crazy Mixer. Your generated voice becomes the main sound.
  3. 3Add one or two sounds. Place them before, during, or after the voice and adjust volume, length, fades, and start time.
  4. 4Click APPLY CRAZY MIX & PLAY to hear the voice and added sounds together.
  5. 5Download the finished mix. The WAV or MP3 includes the voice and the sounds you added.
Remember: the normal PLAY VOICE button plays the voice by itself. APPLY CRAZY MIX & PLAY creates the combined production.
🔊

A SOUND BY ITSELF

CATEGORY → SOUND → CRAZY MIXER
  1. 1Pick a category. Animals, Nature, Horror, Music, Vehicles, Mixer Samples, or any other category.
  2. 2Pick a sound. You can play it first or go straight to mixing.
  3. 3Use the Main Crazy Mixer. Change only what you want — volume, length, speed, effects, fades, and other available controls.
  4. 4Click GENERATE / PLAY or the current mix button to hear your changes.
  5. 5Download it when it sounds the way you want.
No need to overthink it. The default settings are already usable. Change one control, five controls, or none at all.

🎬 SCENE BUILDER

This is where separate sounds become a production.

🎬

BUILD A FULL STORY

Add clips → arrange them → overlap them → play the whole thing → download it.

➕
ADD A MIXAdd the sound or finished mix you are already working on.
📁
ADD YOUR CLIPSBring in MP3, WAV, OGG, M4A, AAC, and other supported audio from your computer.
⏱️
PLACE ITUse START / JOIN AT or Auto Join to decide exactly when a scene begins.
🎚️
SHAPE ITSet each clip's volume, fade in, fade out, and move scenes earlier or later.
🎞️
FINISH ITPlay the full story and download the completed production as WAV or MP3.
Why Scene Builder is different: clips do not have to wait for the previous clip to finish. They can overlap. That means music can continue under a voice, an explosion can interrupt a countdown, or quiet animal sounds can sit farther in the background. Scene Builder supports up to 20 clips, and a story can be built up to about 15 minutes.
There is no “correct” way to use the Lab. 😄

Make a clean voiceover. Build a zoo. Destroy a city with a missile warning. Put a dog in the middle of a serious announcement. The controls are tools — the fun part is deciding what happens next.
