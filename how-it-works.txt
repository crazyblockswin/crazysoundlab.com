    CRAZY VOICE AND SOUND LAB — HOW IT WORKS

    There are two simple ways to use the studio.

    ============================================================
    1) CREATE A VOICE + ADD A SOUND OR MUSIC
    ============================================================

    STEP 1 — CREATE YOUR VOICE
    • Type your script in VOICE LAB.
    • Choose the voice and speed you want.
    • Click GENERATE VOICE.

    STEP 2 — OPEN IT IN CRAZY MIXER
    • Use OPEN TO CRAZY MIXER.
    • Your generated voice becomes the main sound in the mixer.

    STEP 3 — UNDERSTAND THE TWO SETS OF CONTROLS
    This is the important part:

    MAIN CRAZY MIXER = changes the VOICE.
    ADD SOUND TO VOICE = changes the ADDED SOUND or MUSIC.

    For example:
    • Want the voice slower, deeper, louder, shorter, or with effects?
      Use the MAIN CRAZY MIXER controls.

    • Want the dog bark, music, rain, alarm, or other added sound louder, shorter, faded, or starting at a different time?
      Use the ADD SOUND TO VOICE controls.

    STEP 4 — ADD THE SOUND
    • Open ADD SOUND TO VOICE.
    • Pick a category.
    • Pick the sound or music you want.
    • Adjust Added Sound Volume, Length, Fade In, Fade Out, and when it starts.
    • Wait until the selected sound is ready.
    • Click APPLY CRAZY MIX & PLAY.

    IMPORTANT:
    The normal PLAY VOICE button plays the voice by itself.
    APPLY CRAZY MIX & PLAY creates and plays the combined VOICE + SOUND mix.

    You do NOT have to play a stand-alone sound first before adding it to a voice.

    STEP 5 — DOWNLOAD
    • When the mix sounds right, download the finished WAV or MP3.
    • The downloaded mixed file includes BOTH the voice and the added sound.

    ============================================================
    2) USE A SOUND WITHOUT A VOICE
    ============================================================

    STEP 1 — PICK A CATEGORY
    Choose Animals, Nature, Horror, Music, Vehicles, or any other category.

    STEP 2 — PICK A SOUND
    Click the sound you want.

    STEP 3 — USE CRAZY MIXER
    When you are working with a stand-alone sound, the MAIN CRAZY MIXER controls change that selected sound.

    Adjust only what you want. The default settings are ready to use.

    STEP 4 — CREATE & PLAY
    Click GENERATE / PLAY to hear your changes.

    STEP 5 — DOWNLOAD
    Download your finished sound as WAV or MP3 when available.

    ============================================================
    THE ONE RULE TO REMEMBER
    ============================================================

    VOICE + SOUND MIX:
    MAIN CRAZY MIXER = VOICE
    ADD SOUND TO VOICE = ADDED SOUND
    APPLY CRAZY MIX & PLAY = COMBINES THEM

    SOUND BY ITSELF:
    MAIN CRAZY MIXER = THE SELECTED SOUND

    Have fun. Make it weird. Make it yours.
