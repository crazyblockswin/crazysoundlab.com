    CRAZY VOICE AND SOUND LAB — HOW IT WORKS

    There are three simple ways to use the studio.

    ============================================================
    1) CREATE A VOICE + ADD ONE OR TWO SOUNDS OR MUSIC
    ============================================================

    STEP 1 — CREATE YOUR VOICE
    • Type your script in VOICE LAB.
    • Choose the voice and speed you want.
    • Click GENERATE VOICE.

    STEP 2 — OPEN IT IN CRAZY MIXER
    • Use OPEN TO CRAZY MIXER.
    • Your generated voice becomes the main sound in the mixer.

    STEP 3 — UNDERSTAND THE TWO SETS OF CONTROLS
    This is the important part:

    MAIN CRAZY MIXER = changes the VOICE.
    ADD SOUND TO VOICE = changes the ADDED SOUNDS or MUSIC.

    For example:
    • Want the voice slower, deeper, louder, shorter, or with effects?
      Use the MAIN CRAZY MIXER controls.

    • Want a dog bark, music, rain, alarm, or another sound louder,
      shorter, faded, or starting at a different time?
      Use the ADD SOUND TO VOICE controls.

    STEP 4 — ADD THE SOUNDS
    • Open ADD SOUND TO VOICE.
    • Choose Sound 1 and, if you want, Sound 2.
    • Pick the category and sound or music you want.
    • Adjust Volume, Length, Fade In, Fade Out, and where each sound starts.
    • Click APPLY CRAZY MIX & PLAY.

    IMPORTANT:
    The normal PLAY VOICE button plays the voice by itself.
    APPLY CRAZY MIX & PLAY creates and plays the combined VOICE + SOUNDS mix.

    STEP 5 — DOWNLOAD
    • When the mix sounds right, download the finished WAV or MP3.
    • The downloaded file includes the voice and the added sounds.

    ============================================================
    2) USE A SOUND WITHOUT A VOICE
    ============================================================

    STEP 1 — PICK A CATEGORY
    Choose Animals, Nature, Horror, Music, Vehicles, Mixer Samples,
    or any other category.

    STEP 2 — PICK A SOUND
    Click the sound you want.

    STEP 3 — USE CRAZY MIXER
    When you are working with a stand-alone sound, the MAIN CRAZY MIXER
    controls change that selected sound.

    Adjust only what you want. The default settings are ready to use.

    STEP 4 — CREATE & PLAY
    Click GENERATE / PLAY or APPLY CRAZY MIX & PLAY to hear your changes.

    STEP 5 — DOWNLOAD
    Download your finished sound as WAV or MP3 when available.

    ============================================================
    3) BUILD A FULL STORY WITH SCENE MIXER
    ============================================================

    This is where your finished sounds, voices, story clips, music,
    and previous Crazy Lab mixes can become parts of a bigger production.

    STEP 1 — ADD YOUR CLIPS
    • Select a sound or Mixer Sample and click ADD SELECTED TO STORY.
    • You can also add one of your own finished audio clips.
    • Add up to 20 clips to one story.

    STEP 2 — PLACE EACH CLIP ON THE STORY TIMELINE
    Every scene has its own controls:

    • START / JOIN AT = choose exactly when that clip begins.
    • VOLUME = make that clip louder or softer.
    • FADE IN = smoothly bring the clip in.
    • FADE OUT = smoothly let the clip end.
    • AUTO JOIN = place the clip near the end of the previous scene.

    Clips do NOT have to wait for the previous clip to finish.
    They can overlap each other.

    For example:
    • Music can begin first and keep playing under a voice.
    • An airplane can enter while the voice is still talking.
    • Zoo animals can begin before the airplane has completely faded out.
    • Ending music can fade in while the final scene is still playing.

    STEP 3 — SET THE OVERLAP
    Use DEFAULT OVERLAP to choose how closely AUTO JOIN places new clips.
    You can still change START / JOIN AT for any individual scene afterward.

    STEP 4 — PLAY THE WHOLE PRODUCTION
    Click PLAY FULL STORY to hear all scenes mixed together on one timeline.

    STEP 5 — DOWNLOAD THE FINISHED STORY
    When everything sounds right, download the complete production as
    STORY WAV or STORY MP3.

    ============================================================
    BUILD ON YOUR CREATIONS
    ============================================================

    Finished mixes can be used again!

    Combine story clips with other story clips, then add more sounds,
    music, effects, or another voice to keep building a longer production.

    Create a clip. Mix it. Build on it. Then turn it into something even bigger.

    You're not just making sounds — you're the storyteller, the producer,
    and the master of your own production.

    ============================================================
    THE ONE RULE TO REMEMBER
    ============================================================

    VOICE + SOUND MIX:
    MAIN CRAZY MIXER = VOICE
    ADD SOUND TO VOICE = ADDED SOUNDS
    APPLY CRAZY MIX & PLAY = COMBINES THEM

    SOUND BY ITSELF:
    MAIN CRAZY MIXER = THE SELECTED SOUND

    FULL STORY:
    SCENE MIXER = PUTS MULTIPLE CLIPS ON ONE SHARED TIMELINE
    START / JOIN AT = CONTROLS WHEN EACH CLIP BEGINS
    FADE IN / FADE OUT / VOLUME = CONTROLS HOW EACH CLIP BLENDS
    PLAY FULL STORY = HEAR THE COMPLETE PRODUCTION

    Have fun. Make it weird. Make it yours.
